# The product
- One-line pitch: See yourself in a new piece of clothing before you buy it, with only that item changed and no unwanted distortions.
- How it works, step by step:
  1. Upload a photo of yourself.
  2. Pick or describe the item you want changed, or layered on top (e.g. a jacket over your t-shirt).
  3. Peitho outlines it, and you can refine the outline with a brush.
  4. Choose the new item: upload your own reference image, or pick one from our catalogue of trending items.
  5. Get the edited photo back, then hold to compare and share or download it.
  (Do not claim how long it takes; this has not been measured.)
- Key features, in plain words:
  - Only the outlined area changes; anything outside it stays untouched.
  - Precise outline editing with an adjustable brush.
  - Reference images: your own upload or our trending catalogue.
  - Before and after comparison (hold to compare).
- What it does NOT do (so you never promise it): never promise changes to height, body shape or backdrop. (Guardrail for you only; not reel copy.)
- Free tier: first try-on free
- Pricing: £3.49 for 5 try-ons, £9.99 for 20 (live). Say "try-ons", not "edits".
- Where people buy: the web app (app.peitho.app), live Stripe checkout
