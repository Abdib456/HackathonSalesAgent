# Research brief 2026-11-15 (Tavily search + claude-opus-5-5 summary, AI-generated)

## 1. Trends worth riding

- **Small swaps over full overhauls.** London winter style is about "clever little shifts that make your existing outfits feel fresher": a new silhouette, an unexpected colour, an extra accessory. This fits "change one item, keep the photo." [https://www.whowhatwear.com/fashion/winter/london-fashion-trends-winter-2026]
- **The statement layer.** A houndstooth jacket over a white crewneck is framed as "quintessential fall dressing." This is a ready-made demo of the jacket-over-t-shirt feature. [https://www.vogue.com/article/best-celebrity-outfits]
- **Trending items for catalogue reels.** Dress watches, brocade and jacquard, and fringe are all trending. Dress watches also suit our watch close-up proof shots. [https://www.thezoereport.com/trends]
- **Refreshing your profile aesthetic.** People are updating grid thumbnails to curate their profiles. [https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-image-size]

## 2. Hook and format ideas seen working

- **Hooks built for the first 3 seconds** drive completion. Creator-style brand accounts, where founders appear on camera, are growing faster than traditional brand pages. [https://newengen.com/insights/instagram-trends]
- **Casual-to-going-out reveal.** The reveal is timed to the hook, and the reel runs 7 to 15 seconds so it loops. This maps onto our before/after wipe. [https://newengen.com/insights/instagram-trends]
- **Same-position transitions.** Filming the same swipe in the same spot makes the outfit change look seamless. [https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfhxnotBR_p]
- **Detail checks.** One AI ad reel invited viewers to check "the bracelet's shape" and whether details look believable "or a little off." This supports close-ups showing the face and jewellery are unchanged. [https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfqEwmGPn0e]

## 3. What similar apps are doing

- **ChatGPT added a Try On button** to its shopping results on 1 October 2026. Users upload a selfie or a screenshot of an item. [https://idukki.io/blog/chatgpt-virtual-try-on-can-it-find-your-products]
- **Movcl claims to keep "face, pose and background."** It bundles this with face swap and avatar features, so our "only the outlined area changes" claim needs visible proof. [https://movcl.com/blog/guides/ai-image-generator-editor-clothes-changer]
- **Style Fit pitches "no studio, no shoot, no photographer."** [https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yudiz.stylefit&hl=en_US]
- **Retailer try-on adds effort.** Catches with Amiri asks for measurements, age and a selfie. [https://www.vogue.com/story/technology/the-vogue-business-ai-tracker]

## 4. Anything to avoid

- **Overclaiming realism.** Generative edits can distort faces, and models "still generate new pixels." Show the proof on screen rather than asserting it. [https://www.dpreview.com/news/what-you-should-know-before-using-adobes-latest-photo-editing-tools] [https://genimagepro.com/blog/best-ai-model-face-consistency]
- **"Idealised self-image" framing.** There is concern about this kind of editing, so stay with "show your best real self." [https://www.acsh.org/news/2026/11/10/ai-reshaping-how-we-see-our-faces-50437]
- **Anything that looks like "AI slop."** Users are complaining about it in their feeds. [https://www.reddit.com/r/InstagramMarketing/comments/1xrzs71/has_the_algorithm_changed]
- **Licensed trending audio.** Business accounts should check access in-app or use original sound. [https://newengen.com/insights/instagram-trends]
